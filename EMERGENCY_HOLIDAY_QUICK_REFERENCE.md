# 🚨 Emergency Holiday - Quick Reference Card

## For Administrators & Publishers

---

## 📍 Where to Find It
**Events Page** → Top-right corner → **"Declare Emergency Holiday"** button

---

## ✅ Quick Steps

1. **Click** "Declare Emergency Holiday" button
2. **Fill** required fields:
   - Title (brief description)
   - Reason (why holiday is needed)
   - Date (when)
   - Priority (Emergency 🚨 or Low 📋)
3. **Select** recipients (students/faculty/publishers)
4. **Toggle** Telegram (if needed)
5. **Click** "Declare Holiday"

⏱️ **Time**: Less than 2 minutes!

---

## 🎯 Priority Guide

### 🚨 Use Emergency Priority When:
- Campus closure (emergency, weather, safety)
- Security alerts
- Urgent administrative decisions
- Time-sensitive announcements
- **Effect**: Loud Telegram notification with sound

### 📋 Use Low Priority When:
- Planned cultural events
- Routine holidays
- Advance notices
- Festival celebrations
- **Effect**: Silent Telegram notification

## ✅ Required Fields
- ✓ Holiday Title
- ✓ Reason
- ✓ Date
- ✓ At least one recipient

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Be Clear**: Use descriptive titles
2. **Explain Why**: Required reason helps everyone understand
3. **Choose Wisely**: Emergency = urgent only
4. **Add Context**: Use additional notes for extra info
5. **Select Recipients**: Only notify relevant groups

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- **Visible to all** selected recipients
- **Logged** for audit trail
- **Requires permission** (admin/publisher only)

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Button not visible?**
→ Check if you have admin/publisher permissions

**Can't submit form?**
→ Ensure all required fields (*) are filled

**Telegram not working?**
→ Contact IT support to verify bot setup

**Need to cancel?**
→ Click Cancel or close dialog (no data saved until submit)
