🔍 Quick Manual Test

  1. Step 1: Open the application at http://localhost:8081/
  2. Step 2: Navigate to Events page from sidebar
  3. Step 3: Click on Calendar tab (if in List view)
  4. Step 4: Verify holidays are visible with colored backgrounds
  5. Step 5: Look for star icons (⭐) on holiday dates
  6. Step 6: Check Sundays have red background
  7. Step 7: Test "Show Holidays" toggle ON/OFF
  8. Step 8: Test "Google Calendar" toggle ON/OFF
  9. Step 9: Click on a holiday date to see details
  10. Step 10: Open browser console (F12) and check for errors
